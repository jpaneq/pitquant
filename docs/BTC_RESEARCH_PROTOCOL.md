# BTC research protocol V0

Before collection/training, append and pin ResearchConfig with its hash. Current configuration: train1095 calendar days, validation180, embargo7, decision step1; reserved final historical holdout 2025-10-01 through 2026-09-30. No historical features/labels at or after holdout start are exposed by Research routes. This reservation was persisted before any real model training; no real experiment ran. Forward evidence after activation has a separate cohort.

Horizons 7/30/90/180/365D have independent baseline fits. Regression target ln(P[T+H]/P[T]); additionally persist simple return; UP iff return >0. Raw returns are never labeled excess returns. Outputs expected_return, p_up, q10/q25/q50/q75/q90, uncertainty are NULL / NOT_YET_VALIDATED until model validation. No fan chart is invented.

Prepared baseline: training-only SimpleImputer + StandardScaler, ElasticNet and LogisticRegression (optional scikit-learn runtime). No random split, no deep learning, no champion promotion. Temporal folds purge labels crossing validation and apply embargo; long-horizon nonoverlap cohorts are reported, not an invented effective sample size. Both training classes must exist. Defaults are pinned ResearchConfig choices, not tuned from observed results.

Blind replay phase1 persists feature/prediction hashes before future reads; experimental OOS model records are committed before phase2 reveal. Phase2 requires exact matured target price and records outcomes append-only. Revisions cannot replace frozen T0. Baseline experimental point-log forecasts are explicitly exp-transformed; that is not the mean of a simple-return distribution. Quantiles remain unavailable. Calibration bins 50–55,55–60,60–65,65–70,70–75,75+ compare p_up with actual UP, and expected/actual returns are retained.

Historical harness inputs start/end/horizon/feature/model/strategy versions. It needs audited historical PIT snapshots and sufficient complete temporal folds before fitting; no lowering gates. It currently returns BLOCKED_BY_DATA. The prepared prediction harness does not claim a complete strategy backtest or equity superiority: Strategy Lab families TRADE_PLAN_ONLY, BUY_AND_HOLD benchmark, PREDICTION_ONLY disabled, HYBRID disabled. The rule-plan strategy harness reuses Simulation Lab and returns per-trade return/drawdown/R/excess. Overlapping trades are not an investable portfolio curve; portfolio turnover and ablations remain subsequent work. Strategy tests benchmark the full matching T0-to-horizon period, including cash after early exit. Paper outcomes already include realized return/R/MAE/MFE/drawdown and excess vs matching BTC buy-and-hold. Cash return =0. Commission/slippage/funding default0 with COSTS_NOT_MODELED; net cost approximation is not validated execution.

Forward cycle archives real data now, pins an immutable daily feature/prediction snapshot during the close latency window, updates existing BTC simulations, and records NO_AUTO_PREDICTION_TRADE. There are no fabricated retrospective forward snapshots. The initial activation occurred after the October4 close window, so that close is not a valid frozen forward prediction.

Explicit post-mortems are allowed only on closed simulations. BTC additional causes FUNDING_EXTREME, OI_EXPANSION, OI_FLUSH, BASIS_COMPRESSION, LIQUIDATION_EVENT, VOLATILITY_EXPANSION, NETWORK_CHANGE, REGIME_CHANGE are classified by a named user, never inferred causal verdicts. Record objective observations and hypothesis through existing ResearchHypothesis before a new challenger. Gradient Boosting and quantile challengers wait for baseline evidence.

Required tests cover future candle/funding/network invisibility, revision freeze and reverting vintage preservation, target maturity, prediction immutability, holdout, purged365-day folds, fractional/weekend execution, replay, PostgreSQL append-only and two explicit synthetic UI journeys.
